Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some common questions about working with me.

How long does a typical project take?

Brand identity projects typically take 2-3 weeks, while website projects can take 4-8 weeks depending on complexity. During our consultation, I'll provide a detailed timeline for your specific project.

What's included in your design process?

Every project includes initial consultation, strategy development, design concepts, revisions, final files, and ongoing support. I work closely with you throughout the entire process.

Do you work with small businesses?

Absolutely! I work with businesses of all sizes, from startups to established companies. I have packages and solutions that fit different budgets and needs.

What makes your approach different?

I focus on creating custom solutions that are uniquely yours. No templates, no generic designs. Every project is crafted specifically for your business and goals.
